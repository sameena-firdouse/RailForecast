How RailForecast calculates ETA

Live train movement → Historical railway patterns → Real-time operational correction → Random Forest delay prediction → Sequential future ETA forecast.

🤖

Current Model: Random Forest. RailForecast currently uses Random Forest due to limited historical railway data. As larger datasets become available, advanced models such as XGBoost can further improve prediction accuracy.
